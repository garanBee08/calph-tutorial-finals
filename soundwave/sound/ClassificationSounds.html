CLASSIFICATION OF SOUNDS

Based on Frequency

Sounds can be classified based on their frequency. Below are examples showing how different frequencies relate to perception and natural sources.

Infrasonic

Frequency: Below 20 Hz

Description: Too low for human hearing.

Example: Earthquakes and elephant communication

Audible

Frequency: 20 Hz - 20,000 Hz

Description: Range humans can hear.

Example: Music and speech

Ultrasonic

Frequency: Above 20,000 Hz

Description: Too high for human hearing.

Example: Bats and medical imaging
