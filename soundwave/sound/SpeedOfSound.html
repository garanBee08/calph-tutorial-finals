Speed of Sound

The speed of sound refers to how fast sound waves travel through a medium. It depends on the medium’s elasticity and density. In gases, temperature significantly affects the speed of sound.

FORMULA AND DEFINITION

🌀

Speed in Air (T ≤ 20°C)

v = 331 + 0.6T

v — speed of sound in air (m/s)

T — temperature in °C

At 0°C, speed of sound ≈ 331 m/s. Increases by 0.6 m/s per °C.

🔥

Speed in Air (T > 20°C)

v = 343 m/s

At room temperature (~20°C), sound travels faster due to increased kinetic energy of air molecules.

Warm air → faster sound propagation.

⚛️

Speed in an Ideal Gas

v = √(γRT / M)

v — speed of sound

γ — ratio of specific heats (Cp/Cv)

R — gas constant (8.314 J/mol·K)

T — absolute temperature (K)

M — molar mass of the gas (kg/mol)

Applicable for ideal gases under normal conditions of temperature and pressure.
