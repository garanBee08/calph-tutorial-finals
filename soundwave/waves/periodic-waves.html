PERIODIC WAVES

A periodic wave is a wave that repeats itself at regular intervals of time and space. These waves are produced when the source of the disturbance is repetitive, such as a vibrating tuning fork or a continuous oscillation. They help us study wave properties like frequency, wavelength, and amplitude more clearly.

Frequency Formula
